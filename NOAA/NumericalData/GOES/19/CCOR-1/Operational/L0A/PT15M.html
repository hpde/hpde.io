NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (2025). GOES-19 CCOR-1 Operational Level 0A Data [Dataset]. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. https://doi.org/10.25921/7e73-tr25.

ResourceID
spase://NOAA/NumericalData/GOES/19/CCOR-1/Operational/L0A/PT15M

Description

Operational Level 0A image data from the Compact Coronagraph-1 (CCOR-1) aboard the GOES-19 spacecraft. The data consist of separate partial-field white-light coronagraph images corresponding to the outer (T1) and inner (N2) portions of the field of view. These paired images are subsequently combined during Level 0B processing to form a full-frame coronagraph image. The Level 0A images have dimensions of 2048 by 1920 pixels and are provided in FITS format with pixel values in digital numbers (DN). The images have not yet been radiometrically calibrated or geometrically rectified. The data are produced at an approximately 15-minute cadence.

Description

The first FITS extension contains a 2048 by 1920 pixel white-light coronagraph image in digital numbers (DN). Each Level 0A file contains either the outer (T1) or inner (N2) portion of the CCOR-1 field of view. The image data are internally compressed using GZIP_1. The Level 0A images have not yet been radiometrically calibrated or geometrically rectified. The CCOR-1 spectral range is approximately 480 to 730 nm.
