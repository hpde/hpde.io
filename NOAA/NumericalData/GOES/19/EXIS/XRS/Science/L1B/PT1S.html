GOES-19 EXIS/XRS Science Level 1b X-ray Irradiance

NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (2018). GOES-19 EXIS/XRS Science Level 1b X-ray Irradiance [Dataset]. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information.

ResourceID
spase://NOAA/NumericalData/GOES/19/EXIS/XRS/Science/L1B/PT1S

Description

GOES-19 Extreme Ultraviolet and X-ray Irradiance Sensors (EXIS) X-Ray Sensor (XRS) science-quality Level 1b data in daily aggregated NetCDF-4 files. The product contains calibrated 1-second solar X-ray irradiance measurements from four detectors: XRS-A1 and XRS-A2 in the 0.05-0.4 nm band, and XRS-B1 and XRS-B2 in the 0.1-0.8 nm band. The high-sensitivity A1/B1 detectors use single photodiodes; the lower-sensitivity A2/B2 detectors use quadrant photodiodes. Separate corrected quadrant currents, data-quality and invalid-condition flags, observation times, and instrument and spacecraft ancillary measurements are included. NOAA NCEI generates the science-quality L1b product from GOES-R EXIS Level 0 source data using retrospectively improved processing and updated calibrations, and aggregates the original 30-second segments into daily files. This science-quality processing stream is separate from the operational Level 1b product used for real-time space-weather operations. The NetCDF archive can contain missing or flagged samples. NOAA generally recommends the science-quality Level 2 XRS products for scientific analysis.
