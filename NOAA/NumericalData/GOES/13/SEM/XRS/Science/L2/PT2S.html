ResourceID

Description

GOES-13 Solar X-ray Sensor (SEM/XRS) science-quality Level 2 2-second solar X-ray irradiance (irrad) product, provided as archived NetCDF files. XRS-A covers 0.05-0.4 nm and XRS-B covers 0.1-0.8 nm. The product provides XRS-A and XRS-B irradiance, raw detector counts, science-quality flags, and original SWPC flags for both channels. Science-quality irradiances are retrospectively reprocessed using updated calibration and are not reduced by the historical operational scaling factors of 0.85 (XRS-A) and 0.70 (XRS-B). Consult the NOAA GOES 8-15 XRS Science-Quality Data ReadMe for processing methods and caveats.

Caveats
GOES-8 through GOES-15 XRS science-quality irradiances may be affected by energetic electron contamination. Unlike GOES-R XRS Science Level 2 1-minute irradiance products, these data have not been corrected for electron contamination. GOES-13 experienced early issues with an anomalous operating mode and has uncertainties in its XRS-B (long channel) irradiance calibration. NOAA recommends using GOES-13 data only when necessary to fill gaps in observations from other GOES satellites.

Description

Science-quality solar X-ray irradiance in the XRS-A (0.05-0.4 nm) channel at PT2S nominal cadence. The legacy operational scaling factor is not applied; energetic electron contamination is not corrected.

Caveats
A systematic calibration discrepancy remains between GOES-8 through GOES-15 XRS-A (short channel) irradiances and GOES-R XRS-A irradiances. The GOES-16/GOES-15 XRS-A ratio is approximately 1.34; this comparison is not a universal per-satellite correction factor. Evidence suggests that GOES-R calibration is more accurate.
