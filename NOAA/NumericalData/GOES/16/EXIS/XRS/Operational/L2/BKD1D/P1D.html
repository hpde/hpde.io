GOES-16 EXIS/XRS Operational Level 2 Daily X-ray Background and Mean Irradiance

Machol, Janet; Codrescu, Stefan; Viereck, Rodney (2018). GOES-16 EXIS/XRS Operational Level 2 Daily X-ray Background and Mean Irradiance [Dataset]. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information.

ResourceID
spase://NOAA/NumericalData/GOES/16/EXIS/XRS/Operational/L2/BKD1D/P1D

Description

GOES-16 EXIS/XRS operational Level 2 daily background (bkd1d) product distributed as a single NetCDF-4 file spanning the available observation period. It provides one daily X-ray background irradiance estimate and one daily mean irradiance for each of the XRS-A (0.05–0.4 nm) and XRS-B (0.1–0.8 nm) channels, together with daily mean coverage fractions and quality flags. Background values are derived from one-minute average XRS measurements: hourly means are calculated, the minimum hourly mean is selected within each of three eight-hour blocks, and the background is the lower of the central block minimum and the average of the minima from the first and third blocks. The operational data are distributed separately from the science-quality reprocessed series. Consult the XRS Level 2 ReadMe and User Guide for data interpretation.
