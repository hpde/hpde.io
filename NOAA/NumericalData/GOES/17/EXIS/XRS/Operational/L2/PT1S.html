GOES-17 EXIS/XRS Operational Level 2 1-second X-ray Irradiance

Machol, Janet; Codrescu, Stefan; Viereck, Rodney (2018). GOES-17 EXIS/XRS Operational Level 2 1-second X-ray Irradiance [Dataset]. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information.

ResourceID
spase://NOAA/NumericalData/GOES/17/EXIS/XRS/Operational/L2/PT1S

Description

GOES-17 EXIS/XRS operational Level 2 one-second irradiance (flx1s) product distributed in daily NetCDF-4 files. The product provides irradiance in the XRS-A (0.05–0.4 nm) and XRS-B (0.1–0.8 nm) bands from individual low- and high-irradiance sensors, as well as primary-channel merged irradiance values for each band. The processing also includes quality/status flags, corrected quadrant currents, and spacecraft/instrument ancillary information. This operational stream is used for space-weather operations and is separate from the retrospectively reprocessed science-quality Level 2 series. NOAA warns that operational L1b and L2 data, particularly in early mission periods, may contain issues not retrospectively corrected and should not be used for scientific analysis; consult the XRS L2 ReadMe and User Guide for details.
