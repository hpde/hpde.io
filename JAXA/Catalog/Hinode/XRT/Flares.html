Description

XRT Flare Catalog tracks solar flares observed by the X-Ray Telescope (XRT) onboard the Hinode mission. It logs event times, spatial coordinates, GOES X-ray classes, and specific XRT observation details, providing high-cadence imagery and movies for solar research.
