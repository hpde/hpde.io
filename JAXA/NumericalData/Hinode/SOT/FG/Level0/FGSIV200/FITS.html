Description

The Hinode Solar Optical Telescope Filtergraph (SOT-FG) Level 0 FGSIV200 FITS data product contains uncalibrated Stokes I and Stokes V images acquired with the Narrowband Filter Imager (NFI) in shutterless observing mode using 200 ms CCD exposure/readout cycles. Individual CCD exposures are combined by onboard polarization processing to form Stokes I and V images; the 200 ms interval is an internal CCD acquisition setting, not the cadence of the output FITS files. The FITS Primary HDU contains a three-dimensional array with two image planes representing Stokes I and V (NAXIS3=2). The product is identified by DataCode FGSIV200, OBS_TYPE='FG shutterless I and V with 0.2s intervals', FGMODE='shutterless', and GEN_ID=38 in the inspected samples. The FITS keyword TIMESPAN is 3.2 seconds in the inspected 2006 October 23 observations. Shutterless readout provides rapid polarimetric imaging over a restricted field of view, with dimensions varying by observation. The observed spectral line and wavelength offset are indicated by FITS keywords WAVE and WAVEOFF. The Level 0 images precede ground-based scientific calibration.

Description

Three-dimensional Level 0 SOT-FG shutterless Stokes I and V image data acquired using 200 ms CCD exposure/readout cycles and stored in the FITS Primary HDU (HDU:0). The first two array dimensions describe the spatial image coordinates; the third dimension contains two image planes corresponding to Stokes I and V. Stokes images are formed by onboard polarization processing of sequential CCD exposures. The 200 ms interval does not represent the output FITS-file cadence. Images are acquired in selected discrete NFI spectral-line passbands rather than over a continuous wavelength range; the observing line and wavelength offset are indicated by FITS keywords WAVE and WAVEOFF. The arrays have not undergone ground-based scientific calibration.
