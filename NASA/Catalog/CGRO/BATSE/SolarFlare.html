BATSE and GOES Strong Solar Flare Data

ResourceID

Description

The BATSE Strong Solar Flare Catalog compiles intense solar flare events recorded by the Burst and Transient Source Experiment (BATSE) and correlated with GOES X-ray satellite observations.
BATSE Instrument: Aboard the Compton Gamma Ray Observatory, featuring eight large-area scintillation detectors providing full-sky coverage for hard X-rays and gamma rays.
Energy Channels: Measures hard X-ray count rates across four standard discriminator bands (25–50, 50–100, 100–300, and above 300 keV) with a time resolution of 1.024 seconds.
GOES Correlation: Provides soft X-ray background and flare class data to verify and complement the hard X-ray timing and intensity profiles captured by BATSE.
Catalog Criteria: Focuses primarily on strong solar events, often cataloging flares with peak count rates exceeding 100,000 counts per second (cps) in specific energy windows.
