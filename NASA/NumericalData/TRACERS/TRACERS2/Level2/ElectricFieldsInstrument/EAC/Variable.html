Description

The Spin-plane electric field vector, measured by the Electric Fields Instrument (EFI), in the TRACERS spinning frame (TSCS) is sampled. The sampling rate is 2048 samples/s. The sampling rate is the same in both Back Orbit (BOR) and Region of Interest (ROI), but the cadence of one 1024 contiguous set of samples is ~64 s (BOR) and ~0.0005 s (ROI) respectively. Both time series data and frequency spectra of the time series are included. Spectra are performed with a 512-point hanning window, with 7/8 overlap and consist of 256 linear frequencies between 0 and 256 Hz.

Description

Electric field measurements with a sampling rate for EDC of 128 samples/s (ROI), 8 samples/s (BOR), EAC 2048 samples/s (ROI), electric field high frequency (EHF) 20e6 samples/s (ROI). For EAC and EHF the sampling rate is the same as the ROI, but the cadence is 16 s and 2048 s respectively.
