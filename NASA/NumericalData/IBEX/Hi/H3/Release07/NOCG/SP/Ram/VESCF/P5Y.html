Description

The Interstellar Boundary Explorer (IBEX) has operated in space since 2008, improving understanding of the outer heliosphere and its interaction with the local interstellar medium. This data set is from the Release 7 1-to-5-year-average IBEX-Hi map data for the years 2009-2013. It contains ram-directional energetic neutral atom (hydrogen) flux maps in the Variable Energy Spacecraft Frame, with no Compton-Getting correction applied to the flux spectra and survival-probability correction applied. The data consist of all-sky maps in solar ecliptic longitude and latitude for IBEX-Hi energy channels 2-6. Energy-channel FWHM ranges are 0.52-0.95, 0.84-1.55, 1.36-2.50, 1.99-3.75, and 3.13-6.00 keV, with center-point energies of 0.71, 1.11, 1.74, 2.73, and 4.29 keV, respectively. The CDAWeb ProductKey is IBEX_H3_ENA_HI_R07_NOCG_SP_RAM_VESCF_5YR.
