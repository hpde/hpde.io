Description

ICON In Situ Ions; 48-day windows of data from the L2.7 v08 product are collected into longitude, latitude, and local time bins. Mean and standard deviations of each parameter are reported in this file. The IVM consists of two planar thermal ion sensors, a retarding potential analyzer (RPA) and an ion drift meter (IDM).
